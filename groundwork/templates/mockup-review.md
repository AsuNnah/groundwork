Independent ("blind") review before the mockup is final. Each reviewer sees only the mockup and their persona, not the PRD or other reviews, so the findings are honest.
Reviewers are fresh-context subagents (or real people), each with a persona from the PRD's users plus domain experts, e.g. "first-time user", "daily power user", "accessibility reviewer", "<domain> engineer".

**Limits:** AI-persona reviews find problems early. They don't replace a short usability test with about five real users before launch, and their likes and dislikes ("would use it") are the weakest signal.
